      Part A
      Customer Terms & Conditions

      These customer terms explain how customers use the AnQ Movers platform to submit moving enquiries, receive quotations, compare Channel Partners and manage bookings.

      1. Use of the AnQ Platform

      AnQ Movers Ltd operates a digital relocation marketplace connecting customers with independent moving and relocation service providers known as Channel Partners.

      AnQ provides marketplace and technology services, including collecting moving requirements, matching enquiries with suitable Channel Partners, facilitating quotation requests, enabling comparison of quotations, supporting bookings, providing communication tools and assisting with booking-related issues.

      Unless expressly stated otherwise, AnQ does not physically carry out removals, relocation, packing, storage or transport services.

      2. Moving Enquiries

      When requesting quotations, customers must provide accurate, complete and up-to-date information about the move.

      This may include name, telephone number, email address, collection address, delivery address, moving date, property type, property size, floors, lift availability, inventory, packing requirements, storage requirements, access information, parking information, photographs, videos and details of unusually large, heavy, fragile or valuable items.

      The information provided may be used by AnQ and Channel Partners to assess the moving requirements and prepare quotations.

      3. Accuracy of Information

      Customers are responsible for ensuring that information provided to AnQ and/or a Channel Partner is complete and accurate.

      If the actual circumstances of the move materially differ from the information provided, the Channel Partner may need to revise the quotation, service arrangements or price.

      Any material additional charge should be explained before the additional service is carried out wherever reasonably possible.

      4. Quotations and Partner Selection

      Quotations are provided by Channel Partners. A quotation may specify the total price, included services, excluded services, assumptions, moving date, arrival window, payment terms, quotation validity period and any additional conditions.

      Customers should carefully review a quotation before accepting it. Unless otherwise stated, AnQ does not determine the price quoted by a Channel Partner.

      AnQ may provide information to help customers compare Channel Partners, including quotation prices, reviews, ratings, experience, Partner status, services and availability.

      Customers remain responsible for selecting the Channel Partner they consider most suitable for the move. The cheapest quotation may not necessarily be the most suitable.

      5. Booking Confirmation

      A booking becomes confirmed when the required booking process has been completed and AnQ and/or the relevant Channel Partner provides confirmation.

      The booking confirmation may include Channel Partner details, agreed price, moving date, arrival window, services, deposit payment, balance payable to the Channel Partner and applicable cancellation terms.

      6. Customer Responsibilities

      Customers agree to provide accurate information, communicate honestly, treat Channel Partners and their staff respectfully, comply with reasonable safety requirements, use the platform lawfully, pay agreed amounts when due and notify AnQ or the Channel Partner promptly of material changes.

      Customers must not submit fraudulent information, impersonate another person, manipulate the platform, attempt unauthorised access, introduce malicious software, abuse AnQ staff or Channel Partners, manipulate reviews, use the platform for unlawful purposes or interfere with another user's use of AnQ.

      Subtopic
      Payments

      This subtopic explains how deposit payments, payment processing and Channel Partner balances are handled.

      1. Deposit Payment

      When a customer confirms a booking through AnQ, the customer will normally be required to pay an amount equal to 25% of the Total Transaction Value.

      This amount is referred to as the Deposit Payment.

      The Deposit Payment represents AnQ's charge for marketplace, booking and associated platform services applicable to the transaction.

      2. Partner Balance

      The remaining 75% of the Total Transaction Value is payable directly to the relevant Channel Partner in accordance with the Channel Partner's payment terms.

      Unless otherwise stated in the booking confirmation, AnQ does not collect the Channel Partner's balance on the Partner's behalf.

      The Channel Partner is responsible for providing information regarding its payment requirements.

      3. Payment Example

      For a move quoted at £1,000, the Deposit Payment will normally be £250.

      The balance payable directly to the Channel Partner will normally be £750.

      The exact payment amounts applicable to a booking should be displayed before the customer confirms the transaction.

      4. Payment Processing

      Payments made through AnQ may be processed by a third-party payment provider.

      Customers agree to provide accurate payment information and authorise the applicable payment provider to process the transaction.

      Deposit payments may include an additional transaction charge depending on the card or payment method used. Any such charge should be shown separately at the time of booking.

      Subtopic
      Cancellations

      This subtopic explains customer cancellations, Channel Partner cancellations, no-shows, changes to a move and moving-day issues.

      1. Customer Cancellation More Than 48 Hours Before the Move

      If a customer cancels more than 48 hours before the scheduled moving date or time, AnQ will ordinarily refund the booking payment.

      A deduction of 1.69% of the Total Transaction Value may apply to cover applicable payment transaction or processing costs.

      Any refund, where applicable, may be made to the original payment card or by bank transfer.

      2. Customer Cancellation Within 48 Hours

      If a customer cancels within 48 hours of the scheduled moving date or time, the deposit payment will ordinarily be retained as the applicable cancellation charge.

      This reflects costs and commitments associated with late cancellation, including resources committed to the booking and reduced opportunity to replace the booking.

      Any cancellation charge remains subject to applicable consumer law and statutory rights. A cancellation charge must not operate unfairly or disproportionately.

      3. Cancellation by the Channel Partner

      If a Channel Partner cancels a confirmed booking, AnQ will refund 100% of the deposit payment to the customer without applying the standard 1.69% processing deduction.

      Where reasonably possible, AnQ may assist the customer in finding an alternative Channel Partner, help communicate revised arrangements, provide alternative quotations and assist through the replacement booking process.

      The customer's original booking price is not automatically guaranteed to remain available if a replacement Partner has different pricing.

      4. Partner Failure to Attend

      If a Channel Partner fails to attend a confirmed booking, the customer should contact AnQ as soon as reasonably possible.

      AnQ may contact the Channel Partner, investigate the circumstances, assist in finding an alternative Partner, assist with any applicable refund and review the Partner's performance.

      Repeated failures by a Channel Partner may affect its AnQ Partner status.

      5. Changes to the Move

      Customers should notify AnQ and/or the Channel Partner as soon as possible if there is a change to the moving date, collection address, delivery address, inventory, property size, access conditions, packing requirements, storage requirements or any other material aspect of the move.

      A material change may affect the quotation. The Channel Partner should explain any resulting price adjustment before providing additional services wherever reasonably possible.

      6. Damage, Loss and Complaints

      If belongings are damaged or lost, the customer should notify the Channel Partner promptly and provide photographs, descriptions of the damage, relevant receipts or evidence where available and any other information reasonably required to investigate the claim.

      The Channel Partner will generally be responsible for claims relating to services it provides, subject to its applicable terms, insurance and the customer's legal rights.

      If a problem cannot be resolved directly with the Channel Partner, the customer may contact AnQ. AnQ may review booking and communication records, request information, facilitate communication and assist the parties in reaching a resolution.

      Part B
      Channel Partner

      These Channel Partner terms apply to moving and relocation service providers registering with AnQ, receiving enquiries and providing services to customers.

      1. Partner Eligibility

      To participate on the AnQ platform, a Channel Partner must operate a legitimate business, provide accurate business information, maintain required licences, registrations and permissions, maintain appropriate insurance, provide documentation requested by AnQ, comply with applicable UK laws and regulations, meet AnQ's Partner standards and have authority to enter into the agreement.

      AnQ may reject an application where the information or documentation provided does not satisfy its requirements.

      2. Partner Registration

      During registration, a Channel Partner may be required to provide legal business name, trading name, Companies House information, registered or business address, contact details, VAT registration details where applicable, insurance details, vehicle information, service areas, services offered, years of experience, industry memberships, certifications, customer reviews, banking or payment information and other information reasonably requested by AnQ.

      A Channel Partner must notify AnQ promptly if any material information changes.

      3. Verification and Partner Status

      AnQ may verify information supplied by a Channel Partner before or during participation. Verification may include Companies House checks, insurance verification, business identity checks, documentation checks, review verification, industry membership checks, operational checks and other reasonable due-diligence measures.

      A Channel Partner must provide genuine and current documentation. Providing false, misleading, expired or fraudulent information may result in immediate suspension or termination.

      AnQ's verification does not constitute a guarantee of future performance or compliance.

      AnQ may operate different Partner statuses or quality levels, including Approved Partner, Verified Partner, Certified Partner, Premium Partner or other categories introduced by AnQ.

      4. Partner Account

      A Channel Partner account is intended for authorised business use only.

      Channel Partners must keep login information secure, not share access with unauthorised persons, ensure authorised staff follow these Terms, maintain accurate account information and notify AnQ of suspected unauthorised access.

      5. Customer Enquiries

      AnQ may provide Channel Partners with customer enquiries that match geographic area, services, availability, vehicle capability, property type, specialist requirements and other relevant criteria.

      AnQ does not guarantee a minimum number of enquiries, a minimum number of bookings, a particular revenue level or a particular conversion rate.

      6. Responding to Enquiries and Quotations

      When receiving an enquiry, a Channel Partner must review the customer's requirements, assess whether it can perform the move, request clarification where necessary, submit an accurate quotation where appropriate and respond within the applicable SLA.

      A Channel Partner should not submit quotations for jobs it cannot reasonably perform.

      A quotation must be accurate, transparent, based on available information, inclusive of applicable VAT where required, clear about what is included and excluded, valid for the stated period and consistent with the services the Partner is capable of delivering.

      A Channel Partner must not deliberately submit an artificially low quotation with the intention of increasing the price after booking.

      7. Customer Contract

      Unless expressly stated otherwise, the physical moving service is provided by the Channel Partner.

      When a customer accepts a quotation, the Channel Partner and the customer may enter into a separate service contract.

      The Channel Partner is responsible for performing the agreed services, complying with the quotation, complying with applicable law, maintaining appropriate insurance, providing competent staff, ensuring appropriate equipment, dealing with service-related claims and fulfilling contractual obligations to the customer.

      Subtopic
      Partner Obligations

      These obligations apply to Channel Partners using AnQ to receive enquiries, submit quotations and serve customers.

      1. Customer Contact and Data Use

      AnQ may provide customer contact information to a Channel Partner for preparing a quotation, communicating about the move, arranging the booking, providing agreed services and dealing with reasonable post-booking matters.

      Customer information must be used only for legitimate purposes connected with the relevant enquiry or booking.

      A Channel Partner must not use customer information obtained through AnQ to sell unrelated services, add customers to marketing lists without an appropriate lawful basis, sell customer information, share customer information with unauthorised third parties or contact customers for unrelated purposes.

      Customer data must be handled in accordance with applicable data protection law, including UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018 where applicable.

      2. Booking Payment and No Circumvention

      For bookings made through the AnQ platform, the customer will normally pay the booking deposit equal to 25% of the Total Transaction Value.

      The remaining 75% is payable directly by the customer to the Channel Partner, subject to applicable booking arrangements. Any additional charges on the move day are directly payable to the Channel Partner.

      Once a customer has been introduced through AnQ, a Channel Partner must not deliberately circumvent the platform to avoid AnQ's applicable fees or charges.

      A Channel Partner must not ask a customer to cancel an AnQ booking to deal privately, provide alternative payment details to avoid AnQ's fee, move the transaction outside AnQ without permission, encourage the customer to bypass AnQ or use AnQ customer information to arrange an unrelated transaction designed to avoid applicable fees.

      This obligation applies during the relevant booking and for 12 months following introduction, subject to legal review.

      3. Partner Cancellation and No-Show

      Once a Channel Partner has accepted a booking, it is expected to fulfil it.

      If a Channel Partner needs to cancel, it must notify AnQ and the customer immediately.

      Where a Channel Partner cancels a confirmed booking, AnQ will refund the customer's deposit payment in full and no standard 1.69% transaction deduction will be applied.

      If a Channel Partner cancels within 48 hours or fails to find an alternative, it may be charged 50% of the total balance transaction.

      A failure to attend a confirmed booking without reasonable notice is considered a serious service failure and may result in investigation and action under the SLA and Partner Handbook.

      4. Moving Services

      A Channel Partner must provide services with reasonable care and skill, in accordance with the accepted quotation, within agreed times, using appropriately competent personnel and in accordance with applicable laws and regulations.

      A Channel Partner remains responsible for its employees, subcontractors, vehicles and equipment.

      5. Insurance, Licences and Compliance

      A Channel Partner must maintain appropriate insurance for the services it provides.

      Depending on the business and services, AnQ may require evidence of Goods in Transit insurance, Public Liability insurance, Employers' Liability insurance, commercial vehicle insurance, storage insurance or other appropriate cover.

      A Channel Partner must provide current evidence when requested and immediately notify AnQ of any material change, cancellation, expiry or restriction affecting required insurance.

      A Channel Partner is responsible for obtaining and maintaining all licences, registrations, permits and authorisations required for its business and services.

      6. Subcontracting

      A Channel Partner must not subcontract a confirmed AnQ booking to another company without the customer's knowledge where appropriate and compliance with AnQ's applicable requirements.

      Any subcontractor used must be appropriately qualified, insured and legally authorised.

      The Channel Partner remains responsible for the services provided by its subcontractors.

      7. Complaints, Damage and Claims

      A Channel Partner must respond promptly and professionally to customer complaints.

      A Channel Partner must cooperate with AnQ where AnQ is assisting with a complaint and must provide requested information and evidence within the timeframe specified by the SLA.

      A Channel Partner is responsible for handling claims arising from physical moving services it provides, subject to applicable law, contractual terms and insurance.

      A Channel Partner must not discourage a customer from exercising legitimate legal or insurance rights.

      8. Performance, SLA and Code of Conduct

      AnQ may monitor Partner performance using information such as quotation response times, booking acceptance, cancellations, no-shows, customer reviews, complaints, communication, service failures, repeat business, response rates and other relevant indicators.

      Performance information may be used to determine Partner status, enquiry allocation, platform visibility, eligibility for programmes, warnings, suspension or termination.

      A Channel Partner agrees to comply with the AnQ Partner Service Level Agreement, Partner Code of Conduct and mandatory requirements identified in the Partner Handbook.

      9. Reviews, Brand and Marketing

      A Channel Partner must not create fake customer reviews, ask customers to submit misleading reviews, manipulate ratings, threaten customers over negative reviews, offer prohibited incentives for positive reviews or interfere with the integrity of AnQ's review system.

      A Channel Partner may identify itself as an AnQ Channel Partner only in accordance with AnQ branding guidelines.

      A Channel Partner must not imply that it owns AnQ, represent itself as AnQ, use AnQ trademarks without permission, create misleading AnQ-branded websites or social profiles, make unauthorised claims about AnQ or use AnQ's brand after the Partner relationship has ended.

      10. Confidentiality and Data Protection

      A Channel Partner must keep confidential information obtained through AnQ confidential. This includes customer information, pricing information, Partner information, AnQ commercial terms, platform information, technical information, business strategies and non-public operational information.

      Where a Channel Partner receives customer personal data through AnQ, it must use it only for legitimate purposes, keep it secure, limit access to authorised personnel, not sell or misuse it, not retain it longer than necessary, notify AnQ promptly of relevant data breaches and comply with applicable data protection law.

      A Channel Partner must not use AnQ customer information to build an independent database for purposes unrelated to the relevant booking.

      11. Fees, Taxes, Records and Audits

      A Channel Partner agrees to pay AnQ all fees properly due under the Commercial Schedule. Applicable fees may include marketplace fees, subscription fees, technology fees, additional services, promotional programmes or other charges expressly agreed.

      A Channel Partner is responsible for its own VAT obligations, corporation or income taxes, payroll taxes, National Insurance, accounting obligations and other tax liabilities.

      A Channel Partner should maintain appropriate records relating to AnQ bookings, including quotations, booking confirmations, invoices, customer communications, service records, complaints, claims and relevant insurance information.

      AnQ may conduct reasonable checks of Partner information and documentation, and the Channel Partner agrees to cooperate with reasonable requests relating to insurance, identity, licences, customer complaints, service failures, data protection, fraud or other compliance concerns.

      12. Suspension, Termination and Liability

      AnQ may temporarily suspend a Channel Partner where reasonably necessary because of suspected fraud, expired insurance, serious customer complaints, repeated cancellations, no-shows, serious misconduct, data protection concerns, safety concerns, inaccurate information, failure to comply with the SLA or other serious breaches.

      Either party may terminate the Partner relationship in accordance with the applicable agreement.

      After termination, the Channel Partner must stop representing itself as an AnQ Partner, platform access may be disabled, outstanding bookings must be dealt with in accordance with AnQ's instructions, customer information must be handled appropriately, AnQ branding must no longer be used and outstanding financial obligations remain payable.

      AnQ provides the platform and marketplace services. Unless expressly stated otherwise, AnQ does not control the Partner's employees, vehicles, equipment, working methods, insurance, subcontractors or physical performance of moving services.

      The Channel Partner remains responsible for the services it provides to customers.
